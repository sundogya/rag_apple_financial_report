# PART I

## Item 1. Business

### Company Background
The Company designs, manufactures and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables and accessories, and sells a variety of related services. The Company's fiscal year is the 52- or 53-week period that ends on the last Saturday of September.

### Products
* **iPhone®**: The Company's line of smartphones based on its iOS operating system. The iPhone line includes iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone Air™, iPhone 17, iPhone 16 and iPhone 16e.
* **Mac®**: The Company's line of personal computers based on its macOS® operating system. The Mac line includes laptops MacBook Air and MacBook Pro, as well as desktops iMac, Mac mini®, Mac Studio® and Mac Pro®.
* **iPad®**: The Company's line of multipurpose tablets based on its iPadOS® operating system. The iPad line includes iPad Pro, iPad Air, iPad and iPad mini®.
* **Wearables, Home and Accessories**:
  * *Wearables* includes smartwatches, wireless headphones and spatial computers. The Company's line of smartwatches, based on its watchOS® operating system, includes Apple Watch Series 11, Apple Watch SE® 3 and Apple Watch Ultra 3. The Company's line of wireless headphones includes AirPods®, AirPods Pro®, AirPods Max® and Beats products. Apple Vision Pro™ is the Company's spatial computer based on its visionOS® operating system.
  * *Home* includes Apple TV 4K, the Company's media streaming and gaming device based on its tvOS® operating system, and HomePod and HomePod mini®, high-fidelity wireless smart speakers.
  * *Accessories* includes Apple-branded and third-party accessories.

### Services
* **Advertising**: Third-party licensing arrangements and the Company's own advertising platforms.
* **AppleCare**: Fee-based service and support products under the AppleCare® brand providing priority access to Apple technical support and authorized service network for repairs.
* **Cloud Services**: Cloud services to store and keep content up-to-date across multiple devices.
* **Digital Content**: Platforms including the App Store allowing content discovery and downloads, as well as subscription services: Apple Arcade, Apple Fitness+, Apple Music, Apple News+, and Apple TV+.
* **Payment Services**: Cashless payment and credit services including Apple Card and Apple Pay.

### Segments
The Company manages its business primarily on a geographic basis: Americas, Europe, Greater China, Japan, and Rest of Asia Pacific.

### Markets and Distribution
Direct sales through retail and online stores and direct sales force (40% of total net sales in 2025), and indirect channels such as carriers and resellers (60% of total net sales in 2025).

### Research and Development
Continued development of new technologies, licensing of intellectual property, and strategic acquisitions.

### Human Capital
Approximately 166,000 full-time equivalent employees as of September 27, 2025.
